Senior Applied AI Scientist — Intelligent Agents and Data Products

ROLE OVERVIEW

We are seeking a Senior Applied AI Scientist to design and deliver intelligent agents, generative AI applications, and reusable data products. The role combines hands-on AI engineering, data science, cloud data platforms, interactive application development, and clear communication with business stakeholders.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

All qualifications below are required:

1. 3+ years of professional experience building data science, artificial intelligence, or machine-learning solutions.

2. Demonstrated experience designing and deploying retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) applications and AI agents using vector databases, LangChain, LangGraph, and Streamlit.

3. Demonstrated experience implementing LLM-powered or generative AI solutions for data analysis, document summarization, and simulation-driven insights.

4. Demonstrated experience building end-to-end data and AI pipelines using Python, SQL, Microsoft Fabric, Azure AI Foundry, Azure services, Snowflake, and Power BI.

5. Demonstrated project experience building role-based multi-agent systems with LLM-driven decisions, resilient fallback behavior, command validation, testing, and deployment to Amazon Bedrock AgentCore.

6. Demonstrated project experience generating and evaluating synthetic tabular data using CTGAN, Python, PyTorch, Pandas, NumPy, and scikit-learn, including statistical-fidelity, machine-learning-utility, and privacy evaluation.

7. Demonstrated ability to convert prototypes into scalable, reusable data products, interactive applications, dashboards, or executive-ready demonstrations.

RESPONSIBILITIES

- Design, build, and evaluate RAG applications, intelligent agents, and generative AI workflows.
- Develop Python and SQL pipelines that integrate enterprise data from lakes, warehouses, and cloud services.
- Build user-friendly Streamlit applications and Power BI dashboards.
- Develop reusable data products using Microsoft Fabric, Azure AI Foundry, Snowflake, and Azure services.
- Prototype and test multi-agent workflows, including validation, error recovery, and safe fallback behavior.
- Apply statistical and machine-learning methods to assess model and synthetic-data quality.
- Translate technical prototypes into reliable solutions and communicate results through clear demonstrations and data storytelling.
- Collaborate with engineering, product, and business stakeholders while documenting technical decisions and limitations.

EDUCATION

Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, or a closely related field.
